**Consolidated Competitor Overview for B.C HVAC**  

---

### **Company Overview**  
B.C HVAC is a residential and commercial HVAC service provider based in British Columbia, offering heating, cooling, air quality, and maintenance solutions. The company emphasizes transparency, 24/7 emergency service, and no monthly contracts. Its website structure focuses on services, emergency support, and tools like the Priority One App for booking and rebate tracking.  

---

### **Products and Services**  
- **Residential**: Furnace, boiler, gas fireplace, heat pump, air conditioning, dryer vent cleaning, duct cleaning, water heating.  
- **Commercial**: RTU (Rooftop Unit) maintenance and estimates.  
- **Key Services**: Inspections, repairs, installations, maintenance, rebate resources, and emergency HVAC.  
- **Rebate-Focused**: Eligible for government-backed programs (e.g., National Heat Pump Rebate), retroactive rebates for 2027 installations, and income-qualified tiers ($4,000–$13,000 for heat pumps).  

---

### **Pricing, Offers, and Promotions**  
- **Standard Residential Service**: $99 (Lower Mainland, Fraser Valley, Sea-to-Sky); $179 (outside).  
- **Add-ons**: $49–$89 (regional variations).  
- **Unlimited Duct Cleaning**: $289 (within regions); $429 (outside).  
- **Dryer Vent Cleaning**: $189 (within); $229 (outside).  
- **Commercial RTU Service**: $289 (within); $189 estimate.  
- **Priority One Membership**: Free (10% off services, referral savings, 24/7 emergency access).  
- **Heat Pump Rebates**: Up to $10,000–$13,000 (income-qualified), $1,500–$4,000 for replacing electric heating, and $50–$150 for maintenance.  
- **Promotions**: Free in-home estimates, no app/installation fees, and rebate eligibility checks.
