**Consolidated Competitor Overview: Sundawn Integrated Services**  

**Company Overview**  
Sundawn Integrated Services is a family-owned HVAC and mechanical services provider with 67 years of experience since 1957. Headquartered in Vancouver and Mississauga, it operates regional offices in British Columbia (Vancouver) and Ontario (Mississauga/St. Catharines, GTA/Niagara). The company emphasizes reliability, long-term client focus, and nationwide service via a network of 350+ licensed technicians.  

**Products and Services**  
The company offers a comprehensive range of HVAC and mechanical services, including:  
- Installation, maintenance (preventative, emergency), and replacement of HVAC systems  
- Boiler service (gas fitter, hydronic heating)  
- Heat pump loops and cooling tower services  
- IAQ/HRV solutions (mold, pollution control)  
- Refrigeration systems (commercial, restaurant, lab, IT/data center cooling)  
- VRF/VRV multi-zone systems  
- Mechanical contracting and sheet metal fabrication  
- Server room cooling  
- Refrigerant reclamation/recycling  
- Cooling tower inspection, maintenance, and repair (cleaning, vibration analysis, seismic corrections)  

**Geographic Coverage**  
Sundawn serves clients across Canada, with regional offices in Vancouver (BC) and Mississauga, ON. It provides national toll-free service (1-877-250-HVAC) and localized support through regional teams.  

**Target Customers**  
The company targets commercial, retail, industrial, and institutional clients, including property management firms, retailers, large facilities, healthcare providers, and food storage businesses.  

**Customer Acquisition Strategy**  
Sundawn focuses on 24/7 emergency services, tailored maintenance/installation solutions, and nationwide coverage via regional offices. Its approach emphasizes rapid response, minimal disruption during projects, and specialized expertise in niche areas like server room cooling and refrigerant recycling.
